Concrete, in numbers

A few things worth knowing about the material that built the modern world.

#2

India is the world's second-largest cement producer, behind only China — the industry underpins nearly every road, home, and structure built here.

2nd

Water aside, concrete is the most-used man-made material on Earth by volume — more than steel, plastic, and aluminium put together.

28

Concrete keeps gaining strength for years, but the industry standard "full strength" test is always taken at 28 days of curing.

1824

"Portland" cement was named by its English inventor for its resemblance, once set, to the natural stone quarried on the Isle of Portland.

50kg

The standard cement bag weight across the Indian market — a unit every mason and site engineer estimates by instinct.

~2000

The Roman Pantheon's unreinforced concrete dome, poured nearly two millennia ago, is still the largest of its kind standing today.

Names that built the industry

A quick look at the houses behind the bags moving through yards like this one every day.

UltraTech CementAditya Birla Group

India's largest cement manufacturer by capacity, with plants spanning the country and a footprint that reaches into the Middle East and Africa.

ACC LimitedEst. 1936

One of India's oldest cement names — its concrete went into landmark early infrastructure decades before "branded cement" was even a category.

Ambuja CementsEst. 1983

Built its name on fuel-efficient plant design and became one of the country's most recognised cement brands among retail buyers.

Shree CementEst. 1979

Grew from a single Rajasthan plant into one of India's most efficient large-scale producers, known for its energy-conscious operations.

Ramco CementsSouth India

A South-focused major with a strong presence across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka — a familiar name on yards like this one.
